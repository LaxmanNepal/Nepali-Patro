How to play
  • There are 4 tigers and 20 goats.
  • Goats enter the board one at a time.
  • A tiger moves to an adjacent point or captures a goat by jumping over it to an empty point.
  • After all goats are placed, goats move one step along connected lines.
  • Tigers win after capturing 5 goats. Goats win when all tigers are blocked.
Tap a piece, then tap a highlighted destination. On mobile, the board automatically scales to the screen.
